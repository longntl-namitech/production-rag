Staff handbook

This handbook covers leave and expenses for staff of the imaginary Harbor Lane Studio.

Leave

Full-time staff get 18 days of annual leave each year. Leave is booked in the team calendar and approved by the line manager. Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.

Sick leave is paid for up to 10 days a year. A doctor's note is needed after 3 days in a row.

Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours staff set their own schedule with their line manager. Working from home is allowed up to 3 days a week; tell the team in the calendar the day before.

Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase, with a receipt attached. Claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.
